# antislop audit 001 (2026-10-30)

Scope: the Vue frontend (`frontend/src`), audited after the build. Mode: AFTER.
Nothing has been changed yet. Pick the numbers to fix; numbers not picked are
left as they are.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM,
Quality Locks = LOW.

## How the evidence was gathered

- WCAG contrast computed for every color token pair, light and dark.
- Headless Chrome (Playwright) driving the running app: keyboard-only
  navigation, every dialog, the profile form, a throttled network to see
  loading states, a 390px touch viewport for tap targets, and dark mode.
- Source search for em dashes, focus-outline removal and control sizes.

## What passed (no action needed)

- No fake statistics, testimonials or claims (R-17, R-18, R-36). Seed users
  are labeled "Demo accounts" on the login page.
- Every navigation item and button has a real behavior (R-24, R-26).
- No gradients, glow, glassmorphism or background patterns (R-01, R-07, R-10, R-13).
- Shadows only on elements that float above the page: dialogs, popovers,
  toasts (R-12). Radii are small and consistent, nothing is pill-shaped
  except reaction chips and the unread badge (R-11).
- No decorative emoji in UI copy; emoji appear only as message content and
  as reaction choices (R-04).
- Specific CTAs: "Create account", "Join channel", "Send invitation" (R-15).
- No horizontal overflow at 390px (R-03, partial), no console errors
  during the click-through, and dark mode renders without broken styles
  (R-34).
- Empty and error states exist for messages, search, channels, workspaces
  and invitations (R-27, partial).

## Delivery Gate status

**FAIL.** Items 1 to 10 are Hard Gate failures, so the UI should not be
treated as finished until they are fixed. Items 9 and 15 are best resolved
together: once you give a direction, I write it into `DESIGN.md` with the
dials and the one-line reasons.
