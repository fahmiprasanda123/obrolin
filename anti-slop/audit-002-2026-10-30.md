## Found and fixed along the way

- **Own presence shown as offline.** Your own "came online" broadcast fired
  before the presence subscription existed, so you saw yourself offline until
  the next event. The presence subscription now marks you online when it
  connects. Verified with two users in separate browsers.
- On touch screens the emoji picker could open partly off screen on the left.
  It now anchors to the toolbar's right edge (measured x=41, width 284 at
  390px).
